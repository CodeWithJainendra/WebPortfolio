Digital Health Vertical

Verified health professionals + your ABHA health records

LocalFixer is expanding into Digital Health. Verified doctors and health professionals (nurses, physiotherapists, home-care providers) can offer teleconsultation and home-care services on the platform. To make care continuous and paperless, users can link their ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) and share their health records with a chosen provider — only with explicit, revocable consent.

How we use ABHA / ABDM

ABDM integration scope

LocalFixer's Digital Health vertical integrates with the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) building blocks to enable secure, consent-driven exchange of health information. We are applying for ABDM Sandbox access to build and validate the following flows end-to-end:

  • ABHA Creation & Verification — let users create a new ABHA or link and verify an existing ABHA number/address within the app.
  • Personal Health Records (PHR) — allow users to view and manage their longitudinal health records in the app as a Health Information User (HIU).
  • Consent-based Health Record Linking (HIP / HIU) — raise consent requests and, on user approval, fetch and share encrypted health records with the selected verified provider.

Create / Link ABHA

User creates or links and verifies their ABHA in the app.

Request Consent

App raises a time-bound consent request for specific record types.

User Approves

User explicitly approves on their ABHA/consent manager.

Secure Exchange

Records are exchanged encrypted, used only for the consented purpose, and consent can be revoked anytime.

Privacy & Data Protection

Your health data, your control

  • We do not store your full Aadhaar number — only verified status and masked details (last 4 digits) where required.
  • Health records are encrypted in transit and at rest, and are accessed strictly on a consent-driven basis.
  • Consent is explicit, time-bound, purpose-limited and revocable by the user at any time.
  • We follow the principle of data minimisation and the ABDM Health Data Management Policy.
